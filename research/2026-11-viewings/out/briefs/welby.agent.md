### Verdict
Pursue only at a deep cut: your extension plan pays only up to £220k; the model says £245k; they paid £242k.

### Price
- **Open £210k**: comparables thin; no bungalow sale on the street since 2021; 78 days, no cut. Rise £216k, £219k.
- **Walk away £220k**: where the extension stops paying; the old £235k loses money.

### Levers
- Unsold 78 days, no cut [L].
- Model: £245k–£251k, £14–20k under asking [value].
- Asks £4,417/m²; Kennedy Road, a bigger detached bungalow, asks £3,889/m² [L].
- 2021 price was the street's highest cash £/m² for a bungalow [value].
- Five-year fixes average 6.00% [market].

### Ask
- Offers so far? Why selling, by when?
- Sewer under the plot: public? Build-over agreement?
- Work since 2021: sign-off, FENSA, Gas Safe, EICR, energy certificate?
- Ever flooded from drains or road run-off? Flood claim?
- Subsidence, cracking or mining claim? Knotweed near?
- Roof age? Workshop roof cement sheet?

### Check
- Loft: W-shaped trusses make going up dearer.
- Manholes: where the sewer runs against your extension.
- Retaining walls: leaning, bulging, no drain holes.
- Walls: stepped cracks over 3 mm.
- Workshop roof: grey corrugated sheet, possible asbestos.
- Drive: one small car only? Shared with No. 52?
- Measure every room against 60 m².

### Red flags
- Hall: you enter straight into a room. Notes say porch into hall; see.
- Lease: anything but freehold; get it in writing.
- Structure: cracks over 3 mm, walls leaning in, the marble rolls.

### Never say
£220k, their £242k or 2026 loan, the 78 days, Lees Hall (also Staves), or your favourite.
