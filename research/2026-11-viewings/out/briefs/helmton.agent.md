### Verdict
Pursue only at a deep cut: done up, nothing to add; asking tops its value; buy test stops at £235k.

### Price
- **Open £213k**: comparables thin; buy test's low end, £34k under the one sale's £247k.
- **Walk away £235k**: buy test's top; under last sale today (£265k) and street top (£300k), after 155 days.

### Levers
- Unsold 155 days, cut twice, 11.7% off £300k [L].
- The one same-type sale, No. 1 (86 m²), gives £3,382/m²: £247k at 73 m² [W2c] [value].
- Market weak: RICS price balance −36; bank rate up to 4% on 5 Nov [market].

### Ask
- Why are you selling, where to, and by when?
- Any offers since June? A sale fallen through?
- What work since 2018, by whom? Send sign-off, FENSA, Gas Safe, EICR.
- Ever flooded, or a flood claim refused?
- Any cracking, subsidence, mining claim or knotweed?
- Freehold, no consent needed to extend? Floorplan and EPC?

### Check
- Patchy fresh paint: damp meter low on the wall.
- Outside: stepped cracks from window corners, over 3 mm.
- Roof, chimney: slipped tiles, sagging ridge.
- Consumer unit: RCDs; boiler age, service book.
- Loft: 2.2 m joist to ridge; W-shaped trusses.
- Side and rear: room to extend; measure every room.

### Red flags
- Hall: front door opens straight into a room.
- Lease: title shows leasehold under 150 years, or consent needed for works.
- Structure: cracks over 3 mm, bulging walls, marble rolls.

### Never say
Any figure; the 2018 £190k; days unsold or cuts; that Underwood and Hollythorpe (Whitehornes too) are on your list.
