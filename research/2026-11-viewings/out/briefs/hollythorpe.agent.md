### Ask
- Why unsold since February? Offers? Sale fallen through? Empty?
- Freehold title in the sale? Any lease: years left, ground rent, consent needed for alterations?
- Works done: building-control sign-off, FENSA, Gas Safe, EICR?
- Ever flooded, or a flood claim or refused cover?
- Any subsidence, mining claim or knotweed?
- Email the floorplan and energy certificate.

### Check
- Measure every room: each 5 m² short of 68 m² is about £20k off.
- Fresh paint on one patch; damp-meter highs low on walls.
- Garden retaining walls: leaning, bulging, no drainage holes.
- Outside walls: stepped cracks from window corners.
- Consumer unit without RCDs; boiler age, service book.
- Loft under 2.2 m joist to ridge, or W-shaped trusses.

### Red flags
- Hall: front door opens straight into a room. See it on entry.
- Lease: freehold not in the sale and lease under 150 years or needing consent. Ask the agent.
- Structure: cracks over 3 mm, bulging walls, marble rolls, doors cut to fit.

### Never say
Budget; the 2010 price; days unsold; that Underwood and Helmton are on your list; your favourite.
