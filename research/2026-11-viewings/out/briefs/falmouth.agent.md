### Ask
- Lease: years left, ground rent and rises, freeholder consent to alter?
- Energy certificate: which floor area is right?
- Offers, why selling, deadline?
- Any flooding or flood claim?
- Subsidence, mining claim or knotweed?
- Conservation area? FENSA, boiler, electrical certificates?

### Check
- Room for a lobby wall, bay and stairs kept clear?
- Measure: under 94 m² cuts more.
- Front wall and bay: stepped or 3 mm+ cracks, bulging.
- Roof, chimney: slipped tiles, sagging ridge, leaning stack.
- Damp: high low-wall readings, fresh paint patches.
- Electrics, boiler: wire fuses, no RCDs, no service book.
- Stopcock: dull grey lead pipe.

### Red flags
- Hall: no lobby fits inside the front door; pace it.
- Lease: under 150 years, or a live covenant dispute; get it in writing.
- Structure: 3 mm+ or stepped cracks, bulging walls, marble rolls.

### Never say
The walk-away, 2012 price, days unsold, No. 33's price, budget, other houses.
