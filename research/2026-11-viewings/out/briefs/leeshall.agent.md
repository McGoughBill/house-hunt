### Ask
- Tenure? If lease: years left, ground rent, freeholder consent to extend?
- Is the extension in the 69 m²? Building-control sign-off?
- Why selling: probate? Empty? Offers?
- Any cracking, subsidence or mining claim?

- Knotweed? Send FENSA, Gas Safe and EICR certificates.

### Check
- Retaining walls: leaning, bulging, no drainage holes.
- Walls: stepped cracks; horizontal mortar cracks (wall ties).
- Extension joint: cracks, damp.
- Measure rooms: over 69 m²?
- Loft: under 2.2 m joist to ridge, or trusses.
- Consumer unit: wire fuses, no RCDs.
- Stopcock: dull grey lead pipe.

### Red flags
- Hall: front door opens straight into a room.
- Lease: under 150 years, or a consent dispute. No answer: see the lease before offering.
- Structure: cracks over 3 mm, doors cut to fit, marble rolls, bulging walls.
