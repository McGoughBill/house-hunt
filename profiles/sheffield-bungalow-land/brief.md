# Scoring brief: sheffield-bungalow-land

Bungalow and land sweep (2+ beds, asking ≤ £325k, all-in ≤ £425k; Meersbrook and neighbours, the Endcliffe Park side of S11, and the bungalow suburbs next to them: Norton Lees, Beauchief, Abbey Lane, Ecclesall, Bents Green). BUNGALOWS (incl. dormer and chalet bungalows) AND LAND / BUILDING PLOTS ONLY: no ordinary houses. The buyer wants one big open-plan living-kitchen-dining space with the front door into a hall, and needs only 2 bedrooms. Buy cheap against fundamentals, refurb or build while renting elsewhere, then live in it. Works budget up to about £250k from family loans. For land, judge the house the buyer would build to the finished spec.

**Vibe:** A bungalow or building plot on a quiet owner-occupied street, walkable to a big park, near Meersbrook / Nether Edge / Endcliffe Park. Not an estate of retirement bungalows, not a student area.

**Finished spec:** 2 good bedrooms (one of 11.5 m2+, ideally a third for an office), one open-plan living-kitchen-dining space of 35 m2+ with good light and no front door into it, front door into a hall, 75-110 m2 inside, all on the ground floor or bedrooms in a dormer. For a building plot: W is the cost to build that house (about £2,400 per m2 plus 12% for fees, services and access), and V is the median of 3 sales of similar new or fully modernised houses or bungalows nearby.

V = finished value: median of 3 done-up same-street, same-type sales in the last 2 years. W = works before contingency. C = buying costs.

## Gates (pass/fail; any fail = REJECT)

- **area**: In one of the search areas above.
- **budget**: Asking at most £325,000; asking + 1.15W + C at most £425,000.
- **tenure**: Freehold, or lease of 150+ years with no live covenant dispute.
- **structure**: No movement, subsidence, knotweed, spray foam, non-standard build (Airey, Wimpey no-fines, prefab concrete) or dry rot. Land: no known ground, mining or flood problem.
- **vacant**: Vacant possession, and no age restriction or occupancy clause (no over-55 or retirement covenant).
- **hall**: Front door opens into a hall, lobby or porch, never straight into a living, reception or dining room or kitchen. Fail if the listing text or floorplan shows it opens into a room, even if a hall could be made later. If neither shows where it opens, pass as unconfirmed and name it in the risk. Land: pass (the buyer designs the entrance).
- **land**: Land only: full or outline planning permission for at least one dwelling, or a garden or brownfield plot with written pre-application support; not green belt without consent; vehicle access and services exist or are costed. Bungalows: pass.
